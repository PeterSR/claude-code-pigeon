# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique code, not people.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, delete, or reject contributions that do not follow this code, and may
ban repeat offenders. Report problems by opening an issue, or privately if the issue itself
would cause harm.

This is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant, kept short deliberately.
